Privacy Policy for CX-Notes

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

At CX-Notes ("we", "us", or "our"), we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit our website hosted at https://cx-notes.cx-apps.site (the "Site") and use our services, particularly when you authenticate using Google Sign-In.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not access or use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

When you log in to CX-Notes using Google Sign-In, we receive access to certain basic profile information from your Google Account via Google API scopes:

  • Basic Profile Info: Your name and profile picture (if available).
  • Email Address: Your primary email address linked to your Google Account, which we use to identify you.
  • Unique Identifier: A unique Google ID token (OpenID token) used to secure your session and prevent duplicate accounts.

Note: We do not request, access, or store passwords related to your Google Account.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Account Authentication & Access: To allow you to create a personal profile and securely log in to the CX-Notes app.
  • Personalization: To display your name and profile picture within your private workspace interface.
  • Communication: To send you essential administrative emails or updates regarding your account status.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

  • No Selling of Data: We never sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. Google API Scopes & Limited Use Compliance

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data only as long as your CX-Notes account is active.

  • Right to Deletion: You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. To request deletion, please contact us at the support email provided below.

6. Data Security

We implement standard, robust security measures (including HTTPS/SSL encryption) to protect your personal data during transmission and storage. However, please remember that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.
