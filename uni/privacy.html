Privacy Policy for CX MultiNotes Viewer

Last Updated: September 19, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to CX MultiNotes Viewer. This privacy policy explains how our application handles your data when you use it to connect to your Google Drive account.

Information We Access

When you grant permission, our application requests read-only access to your Google Drive files. Specifically, we only access files located within the single Google Drive folder ID that you provide to the application. We do not access, read, or interact with any other folders or files in your Google Drive.

The application uses the `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly` scope, which is limited to viewing files and their content. The application cannot and does not modify, delete, or share your files.

How We Use Your Information

The content of the text files from your specified folder is fetched and displayed directly within the application for your viewing convenience. No data from your Google Drive is ever stored, saved, or transmitted to any server or third party. All data processing happens locally in your browser. The application uses `sessionStorage` to keep your authentication token secure during your session and `localStorage` to remember the Folder ID you provided, so you don't have to enter it every time.

Data Storage and Sharing

We do not store any of your personal data or file content. We do not share any data with third parties. Your information remains within your browser and your own Google Account.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page.
